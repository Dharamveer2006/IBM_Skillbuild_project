Key Analytical Insights

Derived from 50,000 student records across all 5 major categories

📉
GPA Change by Burnout Risk
+0.196 vs +0.200
High-risk students show lower average GPA improvement (+0.196) compared to Low-risk (+0.200), confirming burnout suppresses academic performance.
🤖
AI Dependency vs Skill Retention
r = −0.084
Weak negative correlation between perceived AI dependency and skill retention — higher dependency is consistently associated with lower retained learning.
✏️
Prompt Skill is Protective
Lower burnout rate
Advanced prompt engineers show a lower High-burnout rate than Beginners, demonstrating that skill quality mediates AI's psychological impact on students.
⏰
GenAI Hours vs GPA Change
r = −0.047
Weak negative trend: more GenAI hours weakly correlates with lower GPA improvement. Traditional study hours remain a stronger positive predictor.
🏫
Policy Shapes Behaviour
Allowed w/ Citation best
Actively-Encouraged policies produce widest AI-to-study variance. Strict bans don't reduce anxiety. Citation-based policies balance adoption and outcomes best.
🌲
Random Forest Wins
51.87% accuracy
RF beats both LR (51.12%) and the 42.3% majority-class baseline — confirming non-linear feature interactions that linear models miss.

Ethical Recommendations

Responsible AI integration in educational institutions

1
🎓 Embed AI Literacy in Curricula
Prompt engineering and critical AI evaluation should be formal competencies in every academic programme — not optional extras. Students with better prompt skills show measurably better outcomes.
2
⚖️ Adopt Citation-Based AI Policies
Blanket bans neither reduce anxiety nor improve GPA. Policies requiring students to cite and critically engage with AI output strike the best balance between adoption and academic integrity.
3
🚨 Deploy Early-Warning Systems Responsibly
ML burnout predictors like the model in this project can flag at-risk students at mid-semester. Deployment must be transparent, voluntary, and governed by student data consent frameworks.
4
📚 Preserve Traditional Study Time
Traditional study hours remain among the top predictors of GPA improvement. Institutions should ensure GenAI tools supplement — not replace — deep independent learning and problem-solving.
5
🔒 Govern Student Data Transparently
Any deployment of AI behavioural analytics on students must follow data minimisation principles, obtain explicit consent, and provide opt-out options without academic penalty.
